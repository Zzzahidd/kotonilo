# কত নিলো? (Kotonilo?)

---

## What is this?

In Bangladesh, buying something isn’t just an economic transaction. It’s an extreme psychological sport.

The moment you step into your house carrying any shopping bag—be it an iPhone, 1 kg of Hilsa fish, a t-shirt from New Market, or a haircut from the local salon—nobody asks:
- *"How was your day?"*
- *"Are you happy with your purchase?"*

Instead, with hawk eyes and zero chill, the entire room looks at you and asks:
> ### 🗣️ **"কত নিলো?!"**

And no matter what number comes out of your mouth, the universal response is already predetermined:
> *"ধুর মিয়া! পুরাই ঠকে আসছেন! আমার এক পরিচিত দোস্ত আছে স্টেডিয়াম মার্কেটে, ওরে কইলে এইটা অর্ধেক দামে দিতো!"*  
> *(Translation: "Bro, you got totally scammed! I have a friend at Stadium Market who would’ve given you two of these for free and bought you biryani.")*

---

## The Core Problem We Are Solving

1. **The Post-Purchase Anxiety Crisis:**  
   You just bargained with a shopkeeper for 45 grueling minutes, sweating through your shirt, feeling like Wolf of Wall Street because you saved ৳50. You get home, proud. Within 30 seconds, your uncle destroys your self-esteem by saying: *"আরেহ, এইটা তো ফুটপাতে ৫০ টাকায় ৩টা বেচে!"*

2. **The "Bhai, Ekdom Shena Shena Dam" Trap:**  
   Every shopkeeper in the country says *"আপনার জন্য একদম কেনা দামে ছেড়ে দিলাম"* (Giving you at cost price!). Scientifically, 99.9% of the time, they just funded their vacation to Cox's Bazar with your margin.

3. **The Rickshaw / CNG Roulette:**  
   Asking for fare to Gulshan:
   - Rickshaw mama: *"৳৩০০ দেন মামা, গ্যাসের দাম বাড়ছে।"* (Bro, rickshaws don't even use gas...)

---

## What Does *কত নিলো?* Do?

- **Gives you receipts before regret:** Find out what the whole country actually paid before you pull out your wallet.
- **Uncle-Proof Defense Shield:** When your relative says you overpaid, whip out the graph and show them: *"না মামা, বাজার দর এইটাই!"*
- **Anonymous Confessions:** Secretly check if you got robbed without having to confess your financial sins to your friends.
- **Collective Trauma Bonding:** A sacred space where victims of overpriced food carts and high-bargain fashion gather in solidarity.

---

## The Golden Rules of Kotonilo

1. **Rule #1:** Never reveal your real purchase price until you check the app first.
2. **Rule #2:** If someone says *"আমার লোক আছে কমে দেবে"*, check if that "lok" actually exists or if it's just pure family mythology.
3. **Rule #3:** If you paid less than everyone else, you are legally obligated to boast about it here.
